Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Better Thyself collects and uses information when you visit better-thyself-website.betterthyself-og.workers.dev

About Us

Better Thyself is a men's self-improvement brotherhood offering digital products and services, and a weekly newsletter called Iron Sharpens Iron.

Information We Collect

Newsletter

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address and your subscription consent. We use this information to send Iron Sharpens Iron, including occasional updates, promotions, and offers from Better Thyself. You can unsubscribe at any time

Purchases

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full credit or debit card number. When you make a purchase, we may receive information such as your name, email address, and details about the product or service you purchased. We use this information to process your order and provide support when needed.

Messages

If you contact us by email, we keep your email address and the contents of your message so that we can respond to you and keep a record of the conversation when necessary.

Website Information

Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages requested. This information helps us operate and protect the website.

Cookies

Better Thyself does not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. Third-party services, such as Stripe, may use cookies or similar technologies when you interact with their services. Their use of these technologies is governed by their own privacy policies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send our newsletter and related communications to subscribers;
  • Process purchases and provide products or services;
  • Respond to messages and support requests;
  • Operate, maintain, and secure our website; and
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to help operate Better Thyself:

  • Brevo, for our newsletter list and emails: Brevo Privacy Policy
  • Stripe, for payments: Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare, for website hosting: Cloudflare Privacy Policy

    • These services may process information on our behalf and may have their own privacy policies governing how they handle information.

International Processing

Some of our service providers operate in countries outside your own. As a result, your information may be processed or stored internationally.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your newsletter information until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Purchase records are kept as long as needed to fulfil orders, provide support, and meet legal and tax obligations.

Information contained in emails or other communications may be retained when reasonably necessary to respond to you, maintain business records, or resolve disputes.

Security

We use HTTPS and rely on established third-party services to help protect the information we handle.

No method of transmitting or storing data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link included in our emails.

You can also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

Children

Better Thyself is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a request regarding your personal information, contact us at: contactme.betterthyself.og@proton.me.
